03 · Meet Sean

About Sean

Meet Coach Sean.

Sean Leyva is an independent pickleball coach with approximately 1.5 years of teaching experience.

He primarily works with beginners — helping new players get comfortable with the game and build confidence. Sean also has experience working with intermediate players, and currently offers both private and group coaching.

Pickleball coaching is currently a side business for Sean, but he wants to grow it into a full-fledged coaching business over time. For now, that means a personal, friendly setup — you work with Sean himself.
