03 · Credential

Certification

PPR Certified Pickleball Pro.

Joe holds the PPR Certified Pickleball Pro credential from the Professional Pickleball Registry — a coaching credential for pickleball teaching professionals.

For a player meeting Joe for the first time, it's a clear starting point: the coach on the other side of the net is certified to teach the game.

Behind the credential sits 17 years of playing, approximately seven years coaching pickleball, and 20 years of tennis coaching before that.
