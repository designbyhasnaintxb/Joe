Privacy

What this site collects

The only personal information this website collects is what you choose to send through the lesson inquiry form:

  • your name and email address
  • your phone number, if you add one
  • the lesson type you're interested in, a group size (for small-group lessons), and any message you write

How it's used

Your details are used only to reply to your lesson inquiry with Joe Borzello. They are not sold or shared for marketing.

Where it's stored

Form submissions are received and stored by the website's hosting provider (Netlify) so they can be passed on to Joe.

Fonts and scripts

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and animation libraries from public content-delivery networks, which receive standard technical information (such as your IP address) when your browser requests those files.

Changes or removal

If you'd like your details corrected or removed, email coachjoe616@gmail.com.
