Privacy

What this site collects

The only personal information this website collects is what you choose to send through the lesson request form:

  • your name and email address
  • your phone number
  • the coaching type you're interested in, your experience level, what you're looking for, your preferred days and times, how you found Sean (if you say), and any message you write

How it's used

Your details are used only to reply to your lesson request with Coach Sean Leyva. They are not sold or shared for marketing.

Where it's stored

Form submissions are received and stored by the website's hosting provider (Netlify) so they can be passed on to Sean.

Fonts and scripts

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and animation libraries from public content-delivery networks, which receive standard technical information (such as your IP address) when your browser requests those files.

Changes or removal

If you'd like your details corrected or removed, send a note through the lesson request form and mention it in your message.
